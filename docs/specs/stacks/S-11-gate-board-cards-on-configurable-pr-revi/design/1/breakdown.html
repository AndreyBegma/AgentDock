  1. 1

    Review strip on the card face New

    A row between the title and the PR footer. It shows one segment per enabled step: green for passed, teal for running, amber for fixing, red for stopped. The right-hand text names the current step. It appears only once CI is green. Before that, the existing CI dot speaks.

  2. 2

    Fix rounds stay on the card New

    A blocking finding starts a fix round in the same session. The pill reads “Fixing” (paused hue) and the strip shows “Fix 1/3”. After the third round the card shows “Needs you” and “3 fixes used”, the same wording as the CI budget stop (ADR-0035 §3).

    shipping.tsneedsPersonReason
  3. 3

    Finished means reviewed Changed

    A card reaches Finished, and auto-merges, only with green CI, every step passed and the blocking findings fixed. Finished cards keep a green strip with the nit count. When review is off, the strip is absent and behaviour is unchanged.

    ADR-0035 §4 amended
  4. 4

    Review tab in the card sheet New

    Sits after Overview. Its badge counts open blocking findings. The tab state lives in the URL with ?tab=review, like the other sheet tabs.

    TabGroupcard-tabs.tsx
  5. 5

    Gate to Finished

    The project's ordered steps, with CI as the fixed first gate. Each row shows its source: built-in Peer review, or the skill a custom step runs. A row's badge gives its state, and a passed step that must re-run after a fix says so.

  6. 6

    Findings

    Stored findings, filtered with a SegmentedControl: Blocking, Nits, Fixed. Each row shows severity, the title, file:line, the step that raised it, and its fix state. Nits never start a fix round.

    SegmentedControlBadgeStatusPill

    Shipping facts and actions

    Shows the fix budget as a Progress bar, the auto-merge posture, and whether findings are mirrored as GitHub PR comments (off by default, set in Settings › Review). “Re-run review” is a secondary action; the sheet has no solid button.
