Design viewer on a phone

The viewer fills the screen. You get back to the card with one tap at the bottom edge instead of reaching for a small X in the top corner.

Behaviour

  • One tap on Card closes the viewer and returns to the card it was opened from, with its scroll position kept.
  • Escape and the system back gesture call the same exit. The viewer adds a history entry, so browser back does not leave the board.
  • The comments button opens the existing comment panel and shows the open count.
  • The preview's scroll area ends 96 px above the bottom, so the last row of a mockup can scroll clear of the bar.
