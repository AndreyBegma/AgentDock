Phone back bar

A glass bar that floats at the bottom edge, where the thumb rests. It holds a 44 px Back or Close button with a word on it, an optional title, and at most one action. Every full-screen layer and sheet uses it below lg. From lg up they keep their current desktop controls.

Back — returns to where you came from

The label names the destination, not the word “Back”.

Anatomy

Bar
60 px tall, 22 px radius, glass-strong, 10 px side gutter
Position
bottom = max(safe-area, 12 px) + --keyboard-inset; above the layer's own z-index
Exit button
44 px tall (--size-tap), 22 px chevron or X, 15/600 label, raised fill with a line-strong border
Title
optional; 14/600 ink with a 12 px ink-3 subtitle, centred, truncated
Trailing
optional: one 44 px ghost icon or one solid action
Visible
below lg (1024 px) only
Same action
bar button = Escape = the system back gesture
Content
the layer pads its scroll area by 96 px plus the safe area, so nothing hides under the bar

  kind="back"            // 'back' | 'close'
